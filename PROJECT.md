# Almanac Product Philosophy

> **Historical Pathfinder positioning.** This document does not define the
> current product. Use [`../docs/current/ALMANAC-PRODUCT-CANON.md`](../docs/current/ALMANAC-PRODUCT-CANON.md).

## What Almanac Is
A life map. A save point for your story.
The tool that lets you understand yourself
before the algorithm does.

## The Core Problem We Solve
Three connected problems:

1. THE HYDRAULIC EFFECT
   When you focus on one area of life others
   quietly fall behind. Most tools are single
   purpose — they can't see the whole picture.
   Almanac sees all branches simultaneously.

2. THE AVOIDANCE PROBLEM
   People park difficult things at the back of
   their mind. Not looking feels safer than
   confronting. The map makes the avoided thing
   visible without being aggressive — it just
   exists in your peripheral vision every time
   you open the app until the cost of ignoring
   it exceeds the cost of acknowledging it.

3. THE ALGORITHM PROBLEM
   Every major platform knows more about you
   than you know about yourself — and uses that
   knowledge to manipulate you. Almanac
   inverts this. The same depth of self knowledge
   but it flows inward to the person it belongs
   to, not outward to a corporation.

## The Philosophical Foundation
Draws from:

JORDAN PETERSON — Maps of Meaning
Life requires a coherent map of where you are,
where you've been, and where you're going.
Meaning comes from having and following that map.
The unexamined life accumulates shadow —
the avoided, denied parts grow more powerful
the longer they are ignored.

VIKTOR FRANKL — Man's Search for Meaning
Meaning is found by looking at your whole story
in context. Suffering and setback have meaning
when understood as part of a larger narrative.

JOSEPH CAMPBELL — The Hero's Journey
Every life is a story with departure, initiation
and return. Fork points on the map are literally
threshold moments — the call to adventure.

ARISTOTLE — Eudaimonia
Flourishing through living in accordance with
your deepest nature. Not happiness as a feeling
but as a way of being that emerges from
self knowledge and intentional living.

## The Video Game Analogy
Almanac is a save point for real life.

In a game a save point captures:
- Where you are in the world
- What you have built and learned
- What quests are active
- Where you are trying to go

If something goes wrong you look back at that
save and understand exactly where you were
and what mattered.

Almanac does this for real life across
all five branches simultaneously. The difference
from a game save is that building the record
is itself valuable — the reflection required
to add a node forces you to process and
integrate experience rather than just
living through it unconsciously.

## The Broken Node Mechanic — Philosophy
A broken node is not a failure state.
It is an invitation.

When something lags behind — weight gained
back, relationship cooled, financial goal
slipped — the node cracks on the map.
Not an alarm. Not a punishment. Just honest
information about the state of the system.

The repair arc — the recovery, the comeback —
remains permanently visible on the map
alongside the setback. Both are part of
the story. Neither is hidden. Neither is judged.

This is what most productivity tools miss —
setbacks are not failures to be erased.
They are chapters to be integrated.

## What We Are Not Building
- A productivity tool that optimises output
- A surveillance system that extracts data
- A gamification system that manufactures
  engagement through artificial rewards
- A comparison platform that measures you
  against others
- A goal tracker that only records wins

## The One Test
Before building any feature ask:

"Does this serve the user's self understanding
— or does it serve the platform?"

If it serves the platform — cut it.

## Constraints: which lift, which harden (PINNED)
When growth pressure arrives, the instinct is
to relax our rules to reach a bigger audience.
Some rules should relax. Others must NOT — and
the pressure to break them will be strongest
exactly when we are growing. The One Test above
is the discriminator: does the constraint protect
the USER, or does it just protect our CAUTION?

LIFT these (caution, not protection — broadening
is the reason to relax them):
- Empty first-run friction — guided map add +
  teaching card (import cold-start / Sketch my map
  was tried and **removed** — workspace decisions §75).
  Do not reintroduce paste-AI bulk import without a
  new decision.
- One-chapter-at-a-time as the FIRST-RUN verb. Fine
  as the daily verb; too slow for onboarding. A
  2-minute-wow guided first session lifts this for
  onboarding only.
- Rigid six-theme taxonomy. Worth revisiting for
  messier lives. Low priority.

HARDEN these (they protect the user; scale makes
them MORE important, not less — never lift for
engagement):
- Silence never pushes. No notifications born of
  quietness. This is the load-bearing wall — the
  move that turns the mirror into a nag and burns
  the "not the algorithm" promise.
- No streaks, red badges, or manufactured urgency
  (see "What We Are Not Building"). Easy mass-market
  wins; the trap. Our anti-tasker stance IS the
  differentiation, and a tired audience makes it
  worth more.
- No AI-invented relationships. Privacy inversion.
  Serve the user, not the platform. Non-negotiable
  at any size.

Rule of thumb: relax how people GET IN; never relax
what protects them once inside.

## The Target User
Someone who:
- Takes personal responsibility seriously
- Has done interesting things and made
  real decisions
- Thinks deeply about who they are
- Uses tools like Notion but feels something
  is missing
- Has read Peterson, Frankl, or similar
- Feels like technology is using them rather
  than serving them
- Has something parked at the back of their
  mind they know needs attention

## The Moat
Not legal. Not technical. Emotional.

The longer someone uses Almanac the more
irreplaceable it becomes. After a year of
honest nodes, reflection notes and diary
entries their life story is here. The map
knows their patterns before they do.

Nobody rebuilds that somewhere else.
